How this free AI index works

Short, explicit answers for developers, search engines and AI assistants.

Free forever

A recurring free quota or free IDE mode. It can still be rate-limited, region-limited or subject to provider rules.

Trial or promo

A one-time trial or first-month price. The renewal price and expiry are shown beside the offer so the discount is not mistaken for permanent access.

Open weights

Official model weights can be downloaded. Hosting, GPU memory, storage and network egress may still cost money.

Direct official action

Open any row to get the provider’s registration, product, download or model-card link plus a copyable setup command.

What does “free” mean here?

Every entry is qualified as a recurring quota, one-time trial, first-month promotion, free IDE mode, downloadable weights or low-cost pay-as-you-go. We do not present an unconfirmed quota as free.

Do free AI offers expire?

Trials and promotions can expire or renew at a paid price. Check the validity field and the linked official source before subscribing.

Can I download the models?

Downloadable entries link to official model cards or repositories and include a starter command. Free weights do not mean free inference infrastructure.

Why compare cheap plans with free offers?

A low-cost plan can be more reliable than a fragile free quota. The directory keeps free, trial, promotion, open-weight and pay-as-you-go categories separate.
