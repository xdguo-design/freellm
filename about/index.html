为什么创建 FreeLLM?Why we created FreeLLM

我们希望解决的问题The problems we want to solve

信息分散，难以查找Scattered and hard to find

优质的免费 AI 资源散落在各个平台、社区和文档中，用户很难全面、及时地找到适合自己的资源。Quality free AI resources are scattered across platforms, communities and documents.

信息过时，真假难辨Outdated and hard to verify

很多资源已经失效或限制变更，用户需要花费大量时间验证可用性。Resources expire and terms change, so users must spend time checking availability.

降低 AI 使用门槛Lower the barrier to AI

通过系统化的收集、验证和整理，让更多人能够轻松找到并使用优质的 AI 资源。Systematic curation and verification help more people find and use quality AI resources.

我们的收录原则Our inclusion principles

确保每个资源都真实、可用、可靠Every resource should be real, usable and reliable

仅收录官方来源Official sources only

只收录来自官方渠道的免费资源，确保信息的权威性和可靠性。Free resources come from official channels to keep information authoritative and reliable.

区分免费机制Distinguish free access

明确标注免费类型（长期免费、试用、限量免费、学生优惠等），避免误导。We clearly label ongoing free tiers, trials, limited offers and student discounts.

分级证据与真实实测Evidence-backed hands-on tests

官方条件先核验；实际调用过的项目才标记“已实测”，完整评测保留测试日期、环境与原始证据。Official terms are verified first; hands-on and full evaluations are labeled only when they were actually run, with date, environment and evidence.

只发布可复核评分Reproducible evaluations only

没有真实测试就不打分；评分必须绑定测试集、模型 ID、平台、日期和原始结果，不用宣传分冒充实测。No real test means no score. Published scores are tied to a test set, exact model ID, provider, date and raw evidence.
