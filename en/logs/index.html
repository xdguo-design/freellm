Interpreting a scan

New
Newly recorded model, route or offer since a comparable observation.
Recovered
An entry has been observed after a previous offline state.
Offline
A source-observed state transition, not a guarantee about every user or region.
Source issue
Retrieval or verification failed; it must not be silently converted to a service outage.
