Choose a route with fewer surprises

  1. Identify the official model publisher separately from the API host.
  2. Verify supported request format, tool calling, streaming and context window.
  3. Read the provider's free quota, rate limit and geographic rules.
  4. Confirm where prompts are processed and whether your data-handling requirements are met.
