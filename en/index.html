Check before relying on a free offer

  1. Open the provider's official pricing or documentation page.
  2. Confirm your region, account, payment-card requirements and trial expiration.
  3. Check whether the endpoint is actually free today and what quota or rate limits apply.
  4. Run a small request with your own permitted credentials and recheck before production use.

What the directory does not promise

A listed offer is not a service-level guarantee. Provider rules may change, usage may be regional, and a successful source check does not equal a verified inference test. Each detail page preserves source links and limitations, even when some original source material is in Chinese.
