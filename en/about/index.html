Our evidence boundaries

  • Official evidence first: We prioritize published pricing, documentation and provider announcements.
  • Third-party observations are labeled: An aggregator is not presented as an official launch.
  • Free means conditional: Regional restrictions, trial expiration and card requirements can matter.
  • Unknown is acceptable: We disclose missing checks rather than invent availability or performance figures.
