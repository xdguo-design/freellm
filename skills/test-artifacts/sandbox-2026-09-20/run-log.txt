## Interpretation
- E2E means the original or materially equivalent runtime chain actually executed in the sandbox and outputs were checked.
- Artifact means a real deliverable was created and inspected, but the exact upstream Skill runtime chain was not fully reproduced.
- Task means an instruction/workflow Skill was actually applied to a fixed fixture; it is not a program-level E2E claim.
- Partial means only a substitute chain or incomplete portion was verified.
- Blocked means execution reached a concrete missing dependency, network, account, or sandbox-policy boundary.
